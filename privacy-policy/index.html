Who we are

Nikko Homecare provides home nursing, elderly care, therapy and residential care from Hse 128, Nandi Road, Karen, Nairobi. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and to provide safe care:

  • Contact details, such as your name, phone number and email address, when you call us or book a consultation.
  • Care information you choose to share about yourself or a loved one, such as health conditions, medication and care needs.
  • Website data, such as your cookie choice, stored in your browser.

Health information

Health information is sensitive personal data under the Data Protection Act. We collect it only with consent or where it is needed to provide care, keep it confidential, and share it only with staff and healthcare professionals directly involved in care.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange consultations.
  • To plan, deliver and review care.
  • To coordinate with doctors, laboratories or hospitals when you have agreed to this.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Sharing your information

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with people involved in care with your consent, with trusted service providers who help us run our business under confidentiality agreements, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and follow up. Care records are kept for as long as required by the laws and professional standards that apply to healthcare providers in Kenya, then securely deleted.

Keeping it safe

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including restricted access to care records and secure storage.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used.
  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Object to processing of your data.
  • Ask us to correct false or misleading data.
  • Ask us to delete false, misleading or unnecessary data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

Cookies

See our cookie policy for details of the cookies we use and your choices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
