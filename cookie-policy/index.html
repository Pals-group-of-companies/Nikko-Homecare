What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as browser storage, work in the same way. They help a site work and can tell the owner how it is being used.

Cookies we use

TypeWhat it doesWhen it is used
EssentialRemembers your cookie choice (stored in your browser as nk-cookie-consent).Always
Embedded mapsGoogle Maps may set its own cookies when the map is shown.Only if you accept, or choose to show the map
FontsOur typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which does not set cookies.Always

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Your choices

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept cookies from embedded services. You can choose “Essential only”. You can change your choice at any time using .

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking essential storage may stop parts of the site from working.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the cookies we use change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
