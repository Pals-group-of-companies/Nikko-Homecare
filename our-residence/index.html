A calm home, with nursing day and night

Set in a quiet garden on Nandi Road, our residence feels like a family home, with the reassurance of professional nurses on duty around the clock.

Residents have their own space, share meals and company in the lounge, and get the personal, medical and emotional support they need.

Stays at the residence

  1. 01
    Respite staysShort stays that let family caregivers rest, travel or recharge, knowing their loved one is safe.
  2. 02
    Recovery after hospitalNursing support after surgery or illness, until your loved one is ready to go home.
  3. 03
    Longer-term careA comfortable, caring home for seniors and persons living with disabilities.

  • Medication management
  • Personal care and hygiene

  • Help moving safely around the home
